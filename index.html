Forbidden knowledge

Questions from the curious

Is it really anonymous?

Truly. We never show you a sender's identity — no names, no handles, no hints. Senders don't even need an account to whisper through your link.

What stops people from being cruel?

A harsh-message filter with Strict, Balanced, and Off modes, custom blocked keywords, and one-tap reporting. Anonymity for kindness and honesty — not for abuse. Abusive senders can be blocked from your link entirely.

Can I reply to a message?

If you enable "Allow replies" in Privacy & Safety, you can send one anonymous reply back into the dark. The sender sees it only if they return to your link.

Do I need a password?

Never. Veil uses one-time email codes. Nothing to remember, nothing to leak.

Can I delete everything?

Yes. Clear all messages, export your data, or delete your account permanently from Settings. When you burn it, it stays burned.

    The manifesto

    Why the Veil exists

    Some things can only be said in the dark. The compliment you were too shy to give. The apology that arrived three years late. The crush that lives rent-free behind your eyes. Honesty has always needed a mask — we just made the mask beautiful.

    Anonymity is a tool, not a weapon

    We built Veil on a simple bet: when you remove the fear of being seen, most people get kinder, not crueler. The whispers prove it daily. And for the few who mistake a mask for a license — the wards are waiting.

    Your secrets are not our product

    No ad trackers. No selling data. No shadow profiles of your senders — we couldn't reveal them if we wanted to, because we never collect them. The business is simple: a free sanctuary, and a modest offering for those who want deeper rites.

    Built by night people

    Veil is made by a small circle of designers and engineers who all, at some point, sent the message they couldn't sign. This is the room we wish we'd had.

    The code of conduct

    Rules of the dark

    Whisper kindly

    Anonymity is for honesty, courage, and confession — the things you mean, said gently.

    Your wards, your rules

    Filters, keyword blocks, and visibility controls exist so your chamber always feels safe.

    Never harassment

    Threats, hate, sexual content aimed at minors, doxxing — instant, permanent banishment.

    Report what wounds

    One tap seals a cruel whisper away for review. Repeat offenders lose the dark forever.

    If a whisper crosses the line

    Report it from your inbox — our keepers review every report. If you or someone you know is struggling, please reach out to a crisis line in your region. The Veil should never be the only room you can speak in.

    The help chamber

    Lost in the fog?

    I didn't get my sign-in code

    Check spam first — codes come from rites@veil.app. Still nothing after the countdown? Tap "Resend code". Codes expire after 10 minutes.

    How do I change my link?

    Settings → Account → Change username. Your old link stops working immediately, so update your bios.

    Someone is being cruel through my link

    Report the message (flag icon), then raise your ward to Strict in Privacy & Safety and add keyword blocks. Repeat senders are blocked from your link entirely — anonymity never protects abusers from us.

    Can I recover deleted messages?

    No. When you burn a whisper, it stays burned — that's a promise, not a limitation.

    How does the archetype ritual work?

    Twenty questions, four hidden currents. Your answers pull you toward Confessor, Observer, Trickster, or Guardian. Retake it any time from your profile — people change in the dark.

    Still lost? Whisper to the keepers: help@veil.app

    The fine print

    Terms & privacy

    The terms, plainly

    You must be 16 or older to claim a Veil. You own your whispers; we host them. Use the sanctuary within the code of conduct — harassment, threats, and unlawful content void your place in the order, immediately and without refund of any offerings.

    What we collect

    Your email (to sign you in), your handle and profile, and the messages your link receives. We do not collect sender identities — messages arrive with no account, no name, and no fingerprint we retain.

    What we never do

    No selling data. No ad profiles. No reading your inbox except when you report a whisper. Export everything or erase everything, any time, from Settings → Account.

    Offerings & refunds

    Keeper of Secrets renews monthly at $4 and can be cancelled any time; the current cycle runs to its end. This is a demo document — replace with counsel-approved text before a real launch.
