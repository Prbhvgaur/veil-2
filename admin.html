What verification means

the mark of truth ✦
Creator

Public figures and creators with a real audience. Requires linked socials with matching reach, a press kit or platform presence, and identity documents. The ✦ tells whisperers the veil they write to is truly theirs.

Notable

Locally known souls — campus hosts, community leads, journalists. Requires third-party proof (institution letter, org listing) plus government ID. Protects them from impostor veils collecting whispers in their name.

Standard

Any user proving they are a real, single human: government ID + live selfie match. Grants no fame — only trust. Verified accounts get priority in reports and cannot be impersonated without triggering review.

Why the Super Admin door is different ▾
  • Irreversible destruction — only the super admin can permanently delete accounts and dissolve communities. There is no undo; a stolen session means unrecoverable loss.
  • Borrowed faces — impersonation grants sight into a real user's private chamber. The gravest privacy power on the platform sits behind the strongest key.
  • Instruments of law — legal requests carry confidential case material and deadlines; exposure would breach both user trust and statute.
  • The archives — data exports move user information in bulk. One misused export is a platform-ending breach, so release is a super-admin-only rite.
  • Governance of the wardens — promoting, demoting, and dismissing moderators shapes who holds power everywhere else.
  • Everything is inscribed — every login, failed key, and super-admin rite is written to the audit log. In production this gate becomes 2FA + hardware key, 15-minute session expiry, and IP allowlisting; no role can elevate itself from inside the panel.
